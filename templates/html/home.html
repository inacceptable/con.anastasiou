betabud.cloud

A search engine and logbook for Kilter Board climbs — browse the full catalogue, preview any climb on a rendered board, then push it to the wall's LEDs over Bluetooth.

  • Full-text search across 47,000+ Kilter Board climbs, filtered by angle, grade range, ascent count, community rating, setter and benchmark "classics"
  • Every climb rendered on a to-scale board — real wall photos where available, an automatic schematic fallback where not — with holds colour-coded by role (start, hand, foot, finish)
  • Sends a climb's hold pattern straight to a physical board's LEDs over Web Bluetooth
  • Personal logbook: log sends per wall angle and collect climbs into custom groups like "Projects" or "Warm-ups"
  • Pulls the shared climb catalogue directly from the Aurora/Kilter backend, with email or Google sign-in
  • Installable PWA with an offline service worker, deployed on Railway with Postgres

eDiscovery-Lite

A web app for document review and eDiscovery — ingest, redact, code, produce and audit documents, end to end.

  • Ingest & extract text from PDFs, images and EMLs, with OCR fallback and automatic SHA-256 de-duplication
  • Batch Bates numbering with PDF overlay stamps and load-file import/export
  • Click-and-drag redaction on rendered pages with strict burn-in on export and a full redaction log
  • Review & coding workflow with custom tags and per-reviewer queues
  • Full-text search with filters for custodian, tag, date range, doc type and page count
  • Append-only, hash-chained audit trail with per-document chain of custody
