betabud.cloud

01 / live

A search engine and logbook for Kilter Board climbs — browse the full catalogue, preview any climb on a rendered board, then push it to the wall's LEDs over Bluetooth.

  • Full-text search across 47,000+ climbs, filtered by angle, grade, ascents, rating, setter and benchmark "classics"
  • Every climb rendered on a to-scale board, with holds colour-coded by role and real wall photos where available
  • Sends a climb's hold pattern straight to a physical board's LEDs over Web Bluetooth
  • Personal logbook and custom climb groups, installable as an offline PWA
